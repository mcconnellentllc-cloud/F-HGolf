Get Involved

🔗 Join the Website Building Team

You don't need to be a "computer person." Our website is updated with the help of an AI assistant called Claude. You tell it what you want changed in plain English, and it does the work for you.

How our website is set up (the simple version)

  • One online filing cabinet. Every page, word, and photo on the site is stored in a project on a website called GitHub.
  • It updates itself. When a change is saved there, the live website refreshes on its own in a minute or two — nothing else to do.
  • Claude does the typing. You describe what you want (“add this photo,” “change the hours,” “add a new tournament”) and Claude makes the change in the filing cabinet.

How to become a contributor

  1. Get Claude. On a computer, go to claude.ai/code — or download the Claude app from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Make an account. Sign up with your email. A free account is fine to start.
  3. Ask for access. Email mcconnellentllc@gmail.com and tell us the email you signed up with. We’ll invite you to the F&H website project.
  4. Accept the invite. You’ll get an email from GitHub — click Accept. That’s what gives Claude permission to make changes to our site (not just a practice copy).
  5. Start helping. Open Claude, point it at the F-HGolf project, and describe what you’d like changed. Your change is saved and goes live automatically.

Access & permissions

  • To edit the website: you must be invited to the GitHub project (step 3 above). Until then, Claude can only look, not change.
  • For this staff portal: ask the owner for the portal password.
  • Stuck or have questions? Call the course at (970) 774-6362 or email mcconnellentllc@gmail.com.

Don’t worry about “breaking” anything — every change is recorded with who made it and can be undone. Jump in and help!
