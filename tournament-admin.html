Rules & Format

Open public rules page →

Public-facing rules for the Founders. Same page is linked from the tournament card and the Rules chip on the public site. To edit, update founders-rules.html.

Format & scramble mechanics

  • Two-man scramble. Both tee off, team selects best drive, non-selected ball picked up.
  • Both players play from the same location — every shot. After the drive is selected, both hit next shot from the spot of the selected ball. Repeat on every shot until holed. One team score per hole.
  • On the green: mark the selected ball by placing a marker 1 putter-head length from the ball. Both players putt from the same placement off the marker.
  • Full 18 each day; two-day cumulative gross decides flight winners. Shotguns at 8:00 AM and 1:00 PM. Report 10 min early. Flights set by Day 1 gross.

Out of Bounds

  • Hole #1 is OB on both sides. West side white stakes — OB line only runs as far north as the northernmost stake; further north (still to the west) is in play.
  • The exterior fence of the entire course is OB.
  • Penalty: stroke and distance (USGA R.18.2). Play a provisional when in doubt.

Penalty areas — local rule (no grounding)

  • Penalty areas at: between holes #3 & #4, between holes #6 & #7, and the hole west of the driving range.
  • No grounding clubs in these areas — enforced like a bunker (R.12.2b). Grounding = +2 strokes.
  • Relief options (all +1 stroke): stroke & distance, back-on-line, or 2 club lengths lateral from where the ball crossed the edge, no closer to hole (R.17).

Playing the ball

  • Play it down. No fluffing, no bumping. Improving lie/line = +2 strokes (R.8.1).
  • Drops: from higher than knee, within 1 club length, in the same cut of grass as the chosen point.
  • Tree relief (young trees): trunk under 4″ diameter → 2 club lengths relief, no closer to hole.
  • Unplayable (outside penalty area): +1 stroke, then stroke & distance / back-on-line / 2 club lengths lateral (R.19).

Pace of play

  • Target ~4:15 for 18. Keep pace with the group ahead, not behind.
  • Always play a provisional when OB is possible — Rule 18.3. Saves the walk-back and keeps the field moving.
  • Ready golf. 3-min search max for lost ball. Read putts while others are reading. Do not wave anyone through — if a gap opens to the group ahead, step it up and close it.

Ties & committee

  • Calcutta ties → split the pot. Every tied Calcutta payout position splits evenly. No playoff.
  • Flight money 2nd & 3rd → split the pot. Only 1st is played off.
  • Flight money 1st → scorecard playoff (Men's Hdcp order). Hole 11 hardest, hole 2, hole 15, then down the list. First lower score wins. Walk Day 2 → then Day 1 → then committee chip-off.
  • Rules question: call the pro shop / committee, or play a second ball (R.20.1c(3)) and let committee decide.
  • Everything else: USGA Rules of Golf. Committee is the final authority.
