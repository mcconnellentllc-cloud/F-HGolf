2026 Founders Tournament

Rules & Format

F&H Golf Course · Aug 8–9

← Back to tournaments

The Founders is a two-day, two-man scramble. Both players tee off, the team picks the best ball, and both play their next shots from that spot — continuing until the ball is holed. One score per hole, recorded on the team’s scorecard.

1. Format

  • Two-man scramble — both players tee off, best drive selected, both play from there.
  • Continue best-ball on every shot through the hole. One team score per hole.
  • Play the full 18 holes each day (front nine + back nine). Two-day cumulative total decides flight winners.
  • Shotgun starts: 8:00 AM (A.M. wave) and 1:00 PM (P.M. wave). Report to your assigned hole 10 minutes early.

2. Out of Bounds OB

Hole #1 — both sides. Out of bounds runs down both the east and west sides of the fairway on hole one.

  • West side is marked by white stakes. The OB line runs only as far north as the northernmost white stake. If your ball comes to rest north of that last stake and still to the west, you’re in play.
  • The exterior fence of the entire golf course is out of bounds. Any ball beyond the fence line, on any hole, is OB.

Standard OB penalty applies (stroke and distance). If you’re unsure whether a ball is in or out, play a provisional and sort it out when you reach the spot.

3. Pace & etiquette

  • Keep pace with the group ahead of you. If you fall a full hole behind, wave the group behind through.
  • Repair ball marks, replace divots, and rake bunkers.
  • Ties for flight prizes are broken by Day 2 gross, then by the back-nine of Day 2, then by hole-by-hole match-of-cards.

4. Anything not covered here

Play by the USGA Rules of Golf. If a situation isn’t covered above and isn’t obvious from the rulebook, call over the pro shop or the tournament committee — don’t adjudicate it yourselves and slow the group.
